This is who we are...
and what we do!

We are one of the largest and most modern shopping destinations
in the region, bringing together shopping, entertainment,
and dining in one vibrant location. With a wide selection of
international and local brands, spacious architecture,
and a welcoming atmosphere, we have become a popular meeting
place for families, friends, and visitors from across Kosovo.
From fashion and technology to restaurants and leisure
activities, we offer an experience designed for both convenience and enjoyment.













Our team comes to the rescue!

Our team is dedicated to creating a welcoming, safe,
and enjoyable experience for every visitor. From
customer service and security to maintenance and
management, our workers play an important role in
keeping the mall running smoothly every day. With
professionalism, teamwork, and a passion for helping
others, they work hard to ensure that every guest
feels comfortable and satisfied during their visit.
